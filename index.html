About Me

I am a researcher and faculty member with 10+ years of experience at the intersection of experimental physics, electronics engineering, and computing sciences. My PhD at HBNI/IGCAR focused on SiC-based neutron detectors for Indian fast reactors — building semiconductor models from the ground up using Monte-Carlo and TCAD simulations.

As a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa (2020–2024), I joined the Belle II and EIC experiments — designing front-end readout electronics, writing FPGA firmware for Xilinx Spartan-6 and Zynq SoC, and maintaining the iTOP Cherenkov detector at SuperKEK, Japan.

I now serve as Guest Faculty in the Dept. of AI & Data Engineering (AIDE) at IIIT Kota, where I teach Quantum Computing, Cryptography, Applied AI, and Data Science — connecting deep hardware expertise with modern CS frontiers.

Research Focus

Hardware that touches fundamental physics — from reactor cores to particle colliders.

⚛️
HEP Detector Instrumentation
Integrated readout electronics for Belle II (iTOP Cherenkov) and the EIC RICH/PID detectors. SiREAD-ASIC front-end processing 64-channels of 256-anode MAPMTs/SiPMs.
Belle IIEICRICHSiPMMAPMT
💡
FPGA Firmware & Electronics
Firmware for Xilinx Spartan-6, Zynq SoC, PYNQ. PCB design in Altium. TARGETC-ASIC readout for the Hawaiian Muon Beamline calorimeter using Quad-Byte Link & GTP protocols.
VHDLVerilogZynq SoCVivado
☢️
SiC Radiation Detectors
GEANT4 Monte-Carlo modeling of planar and 3D-stacked SiC neutron detectors. Converter material optimization (LiH, HDPE, Perylene) for harsh fast reactor environments at IGCAR.
GEANT4SiCFast ReactorsIGCAR
🔬
Semiconductor TCAD Simulation
SILVACO TCAD modeling of 4H-SiC Schottky Barrier Diodes. Irradiation-effects study — neutron, proton, gamma, electron impact on SiC device electrical characteristics.
SILVACO4H-SiCSBDIrradiation
🔐
Quantum Computing & Cryptography
Post-quantum cryptography, quantum algorithms, and the intersection of quantum information science with classical security. Teaching and mentoring at UG/PG level at IIIT Kota.
QuantumPost-QuantumCryptography
🤖
Applied AI & Data Science
YOLO-based PCB defect detection, AI-powered web optimization tools, and statistical/mathematical foundations for data-driven scientific analysis at graduate level.
