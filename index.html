About Me

Interdisciplinary at the
Edge of Physics & Computing

I am a researcher and faculty member with 10+ years of experience at the intersection of experimental physics, electronics engineering, and computing sciences. My journey began with neutron detector design for Indian fast reactors and grew to include frontier particle physics experiments at SuperKEK (Japan) and the upcoming Electron-Ion Collider (BNL, USA).

As a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa (IDLab, 2020–2024), I developed readout electronics for the Belle II iTOP Cherenkov detector and led FPGA firmware development for next-generation EIC particle identification systems.

Currently at IIIT Kota's Dept. of AIDE, I teach and mentor across Quantum Computing, Cryptography & Cybersecurity, Applied AI, and Data Science — connecting deep hardware expertise with modern CS frontiers.

Research Focus

What I Work On

From silicon carbide neutron sensors to frontier HEP detectors — hardware that touches fundamental physics.

⚛️
HEP Detector Instrumentation
Design and development of integrated readout electronics for Belle II (iTOP) and the Electron-Ion Collider RICH/PID detectors. SiREAD-ASIC based front-end for 256-anode MAPMTs/SiPMs.
Belle IIEICRICHSiPM
💡
FPGA Firmware & Electronics
Firmware development for Xilinx Spartan-6, Zynq SoC, and PYNQ platforms. PCB design in Altium Designer. TARGETC-ASIC readout for the Hawaiian Muon Beamline calorimeter.
VHDLVerilogZynq SoCVivado
☢️
SiC Radiation Detectors
GEANT4 Monte-Carlo modeling of planar and 3D-stacked SiC neutron detectors. Converter material optimization (LiH, HDPE, Perylene) for harsh fast reactor environments.
GEANT4SiCFast ReactorsIGCAR
🔬
Semiconductor Device Simulation
TCAD modeling of 4H-SiC Schottky Barrier Diodes. Irradiation effects study — neutron, proton, gamma, and electron irradiation on electrical characteristics of SiC devices.
SILVACO TCAD4H-SiCSBD
🔐
Cryptography & Quantum Computing
Teaching and exploring post-quantum cryptography, quantum algorithms, and the intersection of quantum information science with classical security frameworks.
QuantumCryptographyPost-Quantum
🤖
AI & Data Science Applications
Applied AI pedagogy including computer vision (YOLO-based PCB defect detection), AI-powered web tools, and statistical foundations for data-driven scientific analysis.
